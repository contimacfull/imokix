## Support
For help, write to the contact below with the app version (About screen), your device and system version, and what happened. Do not send photos or records unless asked; the app stores everything only on your device and the developer cannot see it. Common questions: how to back up (Backup, create the file, then “Save to Files…”; only that button records a backup); why a PDF export is blocked (a signed record failed its consistency check: restore from your backup, or create a clearly marked UNVERIFIED diagnostic copy that must not be relied on as the signed record); why dictation is unavailable (the language has no on-device speech recognition on your phone).

## Privacy
No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code. All data stays on your device. Camera and photo library are used only for inspection and home photos. Microphone and speech recognition are used only while you dictate, on-device only; no audio is stored. Files leave the app only when you save or share them. Your device's iOS or iCloud backup may include the app's data depending on your own Apple settings; the app cannot check or control that.
