App: **Imokix**. Last updated: 2026-11-07.

**Developer / contact:** Alexandru Dan — oadSupport@gmail.com

## Support
Write to **oadSupport@gmail.com** with the app version (About screen), your iOS version and what happened. Do not send photos or records unless asked; the app stores everything only on your device and the developer cannot see it.

Common questions: *How do I back up?* Open Backup, create the file, then "Save to Files…". Only that button records a backup. *Why is my PDF export blocked?* A signed record failed its consistency check; restore from your backup, or create a clearly marked UNVERIFIED diagnostic copy that must not be relied on as the signed record. *Dictation is unavailable* — the language has no on-device speech recognition on your device.
